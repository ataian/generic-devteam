## Repository rules

- **Before edits.** Read `AGENTS.md`, `README.md` and [CAC authoring](references/cac-authoring.md). Apply CAC to new and revised text within the commissioned scope. Check fidelity before delivery.
- **Language.** Use plain technical English for development communication and artifacts. Preserve exact identifiers, quotations, errors and deliberately localized product text.
- **Planning.** Keep versioned planning files under `docs/` outside ignored `docs/superpowers/`. Keep them outside the delivered manifest. Planning-only changes require no bump. Use the SemVer rules in `AGENTS.md` for delivered Maker changesets.
